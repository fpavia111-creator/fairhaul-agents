# Mizar fixtures

Fictional shipments, September and October 2026, all from one Chicago warehouse.

- `shipments.csv`: twelve shipments on three lanes. Three LTL shipments to Dallas left on the same day and would have filled one truck for less; two more on another day come close but do not pay at the default truckload rate. One 14,500 lb LTL shipment to Atlanta should have been quoted as truckload. Two truckloads go to Denver.
