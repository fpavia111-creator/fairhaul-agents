# Fairhaul Detention and Lumper fixtures

Fictional loads, facilities, carriers and terms.

- `charges.csv`: nine detention, layover and lumper charges, each planted with one situation: billed right, billed over, truck arrived late, no dock times, layover not earned, lumper over receipt, lumper with no receipt, carrier override rate, layover earned.
- `stops.csv`: the dock log; `yard-log.csv` is the same log with yard-system column names.
- `terms.json`: two free hours, 50 per hour (60 for Sample Freightways), 15-minute grace and increments, 400 cap, layover after 16 hours.
