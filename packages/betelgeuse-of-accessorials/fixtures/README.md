# Betelgeuse fixtures

Fictional carriers, places and amounts, October 2026.

- `shipments.csv`: nine shipments with the accessorials that were ordered on each.
- `invoices.csv`: their invoices, one row per charge. A home-goods customer in Austin is billed residential and liftgate on all three loads, ordered once; one of those liftgates is above the contract. Dallas is billed an appointment twice and ordered once. Houston has a redelivery, Tulsa detention twice, and Denver an inside delivery that was ordered.
- `contract.json`: accessorial rates for the two carriers.
