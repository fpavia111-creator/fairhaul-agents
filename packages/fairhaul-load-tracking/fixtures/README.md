# Fairhaul Load Tracking fixtures

Fictional carriers, customers and loads. As of time for the demo and evals: 2026-11-16 10:00 (all times in one local time zone).

- `loads.csv`: eight open loads.
- `events.csv`: their tracking events. One load is on time with fresh updates; one has not updated for 17 hours; one broke down and has passed its 09:00 appointment; one was never picked up and has no tracking; one has an ETA two hours past its appointment; one shows delivered but is still open in the TMS; one is planned for this afternoon.
