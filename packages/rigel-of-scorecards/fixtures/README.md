# Rigel fixtures

Fictional carriers, loads and amounts, July to November 2026.

- `shipments.csv`: 24 tenders to four carriers. Ridgeline Freight is on time except one day-late delivery; Coastal Line Haul slips from 67% on time in the third quarter to 33% in the fourth, with one claim and one load with no tracking; Prairie Express declined three of eight tenders and billed above the agreed amount on two loads; Summit Carriers has only three loads, so it is listed but not ranked. Some rows have appointment times, most only dates.
- `scorecard.json`: the default weights and targets written out, so you can see what to change.
- `exports/tms-style.csv`: two rows with TMS-style column names, US dates and currency symbols, to show the column mapping.
