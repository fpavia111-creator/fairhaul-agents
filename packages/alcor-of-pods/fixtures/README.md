# Alcor fixtures

Fictional carriers, loads and documents, November 2026. As of date for the demo and evals: 2026-11-16.

- `loads.csv`: ten delivered loads, nine invoiced.
- `pods.csv`: seven PODs. Two are clean; one notes two crushed cartons; one is unsigned; one shows ten of twelve pieces; one shows a delivery date a day later than the invoice; one matches by PRO only. Two invoiced loads have no POD (one for a customer that requires PODs), and one load delivered yesterday is still within the wait period.
