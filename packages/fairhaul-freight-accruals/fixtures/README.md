# Fairhaul Freight Accruals fixtures

Fictional shipments, invoices and contract (the same contract as Fairhaul Freight Bill Audit). Period end 2026-10-31.

- `shipments.csv`: nine shipments: billed in period (S-1), quoted (S-2), billed after period end (S-3), estimated from the contract (S-4, S-5), no estimate possible (S-6), shipped after period end (S-7), cancelled (S-8), and an old quoted shipment never billed (S-9).
- `invoices.csv`: invoices received, including history on the Chicago to Dallas lane.
- `prior-accrual.csv`: last period's accrual: two now billed (one over, one under), one still unbilled.
